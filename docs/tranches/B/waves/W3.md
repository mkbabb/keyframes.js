# B.W3 — Demo correctness + occlusion-free (inv δ)

The demo is the engine's shop-window, and the W0 design + occlusion audits found it
shipping four blank scenes, a cube that clips off the right edge, a hero that prints
over the cube, and a mobile top dock sheared by the viewport. W3 makes every page
render its subject, in bounds, with zero occlusion on every viewport — and lands the
scripted occlusion gate (inv δ) that keeps it that way. Grounds:
`audit/design-findings.txt` (4 blocker + the high cluster) and
`audit/occlusion-report.json` (home/cube clip 18-20 elements; the other four
"clean" because empty).

## § Goal

Every scene (home, cube, amiga, square, easing, spring) renders its subject visible
and centered at idle, within the work-area bounds, at 375 / 1280 / 1440 — no element
overflows the viewport, no dock overlaps content, no hero overlays the target. The
Playwright occlusion gate (inv δ) is the standing instrument that proves it.

## § Scope

### S1 — The four blank scenes render their subject at idle (BLOCKER)
**What:** amiga, square, easing, spring render a completely empty viewport at idle in
both mobile and desktop (only home/cube show a target). Seed each scene's subject so
it is visible + centered in the stage BEFORE play: SquareScene/SpringTarget/the
easing + spring curve plots render their idle state on mount, not only after an
animation is selected. **Why:** four of six pages currently look broken on first load
— the single largest shop-window defect.

### S2 — The cube never clips; the desktop stage is centered (BLOCKER + high)
**What:** on cube + home at 1280/1440 the cube is hard-clipped at the right edge
(only ~half visible, axis guide-lines bleed past all four edges). Root cause: the
desktop animation stage spans grid cols 2-4 of a `[400px 1fr 1fr]` grid, so its
visual center sits in the right two-thirds even when the 400px controls pane is
closed. Fixes: when the controls pane is closed on desktop the stage occupies and
centers within the FULL viewport (collapse col-1 to 0 / `grid-template-columns`
adjustment); clamp/reset the cube's resting transform so the full cube fits the
work-area bounds on mount regardless of any persisted transform (reconsider
persisting it at all). **Why:** the cube is the flagship demo and it is half
off-screen on the two desktop viewports.

### S3 — The hero never overlays the target (BLOCKER on mobile)
**What:** on home-mobile the start-screen hero ("Select an animation" + "from the
list ≣ above.") prints directly over the cube faces (z-content overlay collides with
the centered cube). On home-laptop/desktop the giant headline overlaps the parked
cube. Define an explicit two-zone home layout: headline column on one side, target
confined to the other within bounds; on mobile, push the cube clear of the hero text
band (park it below the headline or reduce its home-overlay size) so the start-screen
text never overlaps the subject. **Why:** the user's #1 demand — NO occlusion — is
violated most egregiously here.

### S4 — The mobile top dock clears the safe-area (high)
**What:** the TopDock pill is clipped at the top viewport edge on mobile across all
six pages — its rounded top edge is sheared because the top dock has no
`env(safe-area-inset-top)` floor (the bottom dock correctly has
`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`). Demo-owned fix: `TopDock.vue` top →
`calc(max(var(--work-area-top-offset, 0px), env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px)) +
var(--dock-margin) / 2)` (mirror the bottom dock). **Why:** a dock clipped by the
viewport on every mobile page is a visible polish failure.

### S5 — Dock exclusion zones (medium)
**What:** the top dock and bottom playback menubar share `z-dock=40`, sitting above
all scene content — wherever a scene element reaches the top/bottom ~64px band it is
occluded. Reserve dock exclusion zones: pad the scene/work-area by the dock heights
(top + bottom) so scene content can never render into the dock bands
(`--work-area-top-offset` / a matching bottom offset). **Why:** prevents the
dock-over-content class structurally, not per-page.

### S6 — The occlusion gate (inv δ)
**What:** the W0 capture harness (`audit/` — `occlusion.mjs`) is promoted to a
checked-in gate: for every page × {375, 1280, 1440} it asserts zero element
overflowing the viewport, zero dock-over-content overlap, and every scene subject
visible at idle (non-empty subject region). **Why:** inv δ — "no page occludes on any
viewport" — is only an invariant if a gate measures it.

## § Hard gate

inv δ — the scripted Playwright occlusion gate is green on every page × {375, 1280,
1440}: zero viewport overflow, zero dock-over-content overlap, every scene subject
visible at idle. The before/after DELTA (W7) shows home + cube clipping resolved and
the four blank scenes populated, with no NEW occlusion introduced on any page.

## § Folds

Retires the 4 design blockers (blank scenes, cube clip, hero overlay — and the prod
build blocker rides W4) + the high cluster (top-dock safe-area, desktop off-center
stage, hero φ wrap reflow occlusion overlap) + the medium dock-z exclusion finding.
Replaces the chronic Playwright `>50ms`/occlusion stub with a real backed gate. The
dock double-click bug is NOT here — it is glass-ui-owned and routes to B.W5's outward
ask.

## § Design decisions

- **Demo-owned vs glass-ui-owned.** Every fix here is demo-owned (layout, grid,
  TopDock top inset, scene seeding). The dock double-click bug (masked in the demo by
  `always-expanded` on mobile) is glass-ui-owned and never patched in the demo — it
  routes to B.W5.
- **No persisted-transform footgun.** The cube clipping stems partly from restoring a
  persisted transform that can park the cube off-screen; the resolved decision is to
  clamp the restored transform to work-area bounds on mount (or not persist the
  resting pose), so a shared URL/localStorage state can never produce a clipped first
  paint.
- **inv δ runs at every close.** The occlusion gate is the same harness the
  before/after edict mandates, so it is exercised at open and close, not bolted on.
