## Design decisions

1. **W0 is RUN, not authored-now-run-later.** Unlike B.W1-W7, W0's work is
   development that happens *now*—auditing, evidence capture, edict authoring,
   ledger and recap. It produces no engine or demo source, so it does not breach the
   "tranche development only" constraint; it *is* the development half. This is the
   dev/impl boundary B.md and PROGRESS both place at the W0 close.

2. **The lighthouse perf numbers are captured dev-mode and labelled non-authoritative.**
   The prod gh-pages build is broken (the 698-byte shim), so a prod lighthouse run
   is impossible until B.W4 repairs the entry. Rather than skip the audit or report a
   misleading number, W0 captures the dev-mode sweep, records the FCP/LCP honestly as
   dev-server figures in PROGRESS, and defers the authoritative perf measurement to
   B.W4's re-run on the repaired build. Honest-evidence over a clean-but-false number.

3. **The four blank scenes' "clean" occlusion result is a defect, not a pass.** The
   occlusion report shows `clipped: []` for amiga/square/easing/spring because the
   viewport is *empty*—there is nothing to clip. W0 cross-confirms this against the
   design audit (which flags the blank viewport as a blocker) so the occlusion gate
   B.W3 wires asserts subject *presence*, not merely subject *containment*—an empty
   page must fail inv δ, not pass it.

4. **The edict is authored-and-committed, push deferred to the user.** The locked
   decision is keyframes authors the edict and the user pushes (the cross-repo
   write is user-domain, as in A). W0 commits it locally at `8ccf9f4`—making it real
   and inheritable—and records the gitlink bump (`63240e6`→`8ccf9f4`) + the precepts
   remote push as the single remaining step, gated on the user's go. The authoring is
   the canonical advance; keyframes does not push another repo's remote unprompted.

5. **The capture harness output is checked in; the harness re-runs at close.** The
   pinned 6-page × 3-viewport matrix and the captured BEFORE artefacts are the open
