# Pane Audit — Cube Scene
Tranche-J Design Audit | Lane: `pane-cube`

---

## Screenshots Reviewed
- `cube-mobile.png`, `cube-laptop.png`, `cube-desktop.png`

## Composite Verdict

The Cube scene is the flagship demo and it mostly lands: the saturated flat-face cube (magenta/red/yellow) reads as the bold subject against the transparent/checkerboard void, the axis lines (red X / green Y / blue Z) are on-aesthetic math motifs, and the dock pill at bottom-center is clean. However several hierarchy and suffusion defects undercut the identity: (1) the transparent/checkerboard background is a browser-default artefact, not a designed stage — the cube floats on nothing, making the axis lines feel orphaned rather than spatial; (2) the controls pane occupies the exact same visual weight register as the cube itself on laptop/desktop — two competing regions, neither clearly subordinate; (3) the Cube scene title pill ("Cube" + icon) is rendered at the same small scale across every breakpoint — it never commits to the audacious Instrument Serif voice this demo has; (4) the PlaybackRibbon's two-circle `AnimationVisualizer` (the `bg-accent-red` ball scrubber) is the only red element in the controls chrome, and it reads decorative-isolated rather than purposefully connected to the cube subject; (5) on mobile, the controls sheet sits flush on top of the cube with no spatial breathing room once open, and the bottom-row dock button (play icon) duplicates the sheet's primary action with no hierarchy signal.

## Design Hierarchy Assessment

### Eye-movement order (laptop closed)
1. **Cube** (correct — strong saturated color, large 3D form)
2. **Controls pane** (WRONG — should recede; instead it competes due to full-height card mass)
3. **Axis lines** (decorative-read, not spatial — due to transparent background)
4. **Dock pill** (appropriately subordinate)

### Eye-movement order (laptop open)
1. **Controls pane** (WRONG — has shifted to visual dominance: the Play/Reverse buttons, the red scrubber ball, the easing dropdown chevrons all draw focus)
2. **Cube** (subordinate in open state — the 3D subject now reads as the wallpaper behind the panel)
3. **Dock** (appropriately bottom)

**Verdict**: The subject/chrome hierarchy inverts when the pane opens. The fix is making the chrome visually lighter (glass-wash, reduced opacity), not necessarily smaller.

---

## Panel-Open Composition
On laptop and desktop (`cube-laptop-open.png`, `cube-desktop-open.png`), the controls pane and cube are in distinct columns (rail·stage), which is compositionally correct. The **problem** is that the controls card's `surface="cartoon" tier="quiet"` gives it a solid-ish matte appearance equal in perceived weight to the cube itself. The cube is relegated to the right ~60% of the stage and visually matches the left pane in density. On mobile (`cube-mobile-open.png`), the cube is fully occluded by the sheet — which is the correct mobile pattern — but the grab handle pill is nearly invisible (1px sliver) and the sheet lacks any glass treatment, reading as a flat white/gray lift with no depth.
