# pane-home — Design Audit (Tranche J)

Lane: `pane-home` — the landing/home scene (hero typography + cube backdrop + bottom menubar, no controls panel).

Screenshots referenced: `home-{mobile,laptop,desktop}.png`, `home-{mobile,laptop,desktop}-open.png`.
Source files audited: `EditorStartScreen.vue`, `EditorShell.vue`, `CubeTarget.vue`, `AnimationMenuBar.vue`, `ChromeDock.vue`, `design-idioms.css`, `style.css`, `AnimatedText.vue`, `TypingDots.vue`, `scenes.ts`.

---

## Summary verdict

The home scene opens with genuine typographic ambition: the `text-display-mega` Instrument Serif hero is the right register for a poster-first landing. The cube M. Cubert is vivid and saturated, the axis lines give spatial depth, and the `depth-text` cartoon stamp adds the right tactility. The bones are sound.

The headline deficit is **left-half vacancy**: the text block occupies the left portion of the viewport while M. Cubert sits in the right third, but there is no designed *relationship* between them — the text reads as a label floating above the cube rather than as compositional partners that share a plane. The checkerboard glass grid behind them is 10% opacity and 1rem tiles — at display scale it is nearly invisible, a missed opportunity to reinforce the math/grid identity. The subtitle typographic hierarchy is unclear — `text-title italic` at 32.9px reads almost as loud as a display subhead, creating an unwanted two-voice competition below the mega-hero. On mobile, the cube and text collide because the start screen uses absolute top-0 left-0 positioning with a fixed `mt-28` margin rather than a true compositional relationship to the cube's center. The bottom `AnimationMenuBar` sits outside the glass register on home (no animation is selected, so it's just a ghosted dock pill) — it contributes no hierarchy value yet occupies the lower quarter of the viewport optically. The controls-panel "open" state is identical to the closed state for the home scene (no animation is selected, the panel cannot open), so the `*-open` screenshots show no compositional difference — a missed validation surface. One incongruence: the cube face content labels (1–6 `text-display-2`) use raw `rgba(255,0,0,0.8)` face colors defined as JavaScript literals instead of the demo's token system, making them opaque to theming. The TypingDots typing indicator dots pulse at 0.2–1.0 opacity on top of the `depth-text` shadow — on mobile they read as glitchy text artifacts because the dot host and the hero reside on overlapping absolute positioned layers.
