## § Design decisions

- **Integrity precedes polish (W1 first).** C.W1 makes B's asserted-not-met
  gates true BEFORE the design (W2) and dogfood (W3) waves, because a tranche
  that adds polish on top of an overclaimed close compounds the debt. W1's
  real-`<main>` (S1) and occlusion-controls-open (S2) touch the same EditorShell /
  occlusion-gate surfaces W2/W3 do, so W1 precedes them in the DAG.

- **The real `<main>` is structural, not a second `aria-label`.** RESOLVED: the
  landmark must be a layout-box-bearing element — `display:contents` is the
  defect, not the labeling. Promote `<main>` to occupy the grid's center cell (or
  render `AnimationControlsGroup` AS `main`) so its role survives in the a11y
  tree; drop the `aria-label` band-aid (a sole `<main>` needs none). This fixes
  the cause, not the symptom.

- **The occlusion tension is resolved by a manifest flag, not a blanket
  exemption.** RESOLVED: a dock floating over a full-bleed canvas (amiga) is
  intended design; a dock covering the cube/hero content rect is the real
  occlusion. A per-scene `dockFloatAllowed` flag distinguishes them, so the gate
  bites HARD on the real class without false-firing on the intended one — the
  honest resolution of the genuine tension B used to justify the advisory
  downgrade.

- **The two chronics close as ONE subsystem, not two gates.** RESOLVED: the LoAF
  observer (producer) and the >50ms trace gate (consumer) are a producer/consumer
  pair — landing the gate (S4) is what gives the observer its second consumer AND
  closes the >50ms-trace chronic AND the overfitting violation in one artefact.
  The audit's alternative (DELETE the observer) is rejected: the consumer is real
  and wanted, so C lands it. No third tranche inherits "named-forward to a browser
  bench run."

- **π-full is sequenced behind W3's reduced-motion honoring, recorded
  explicitly.** RESOLVED: the animation-timing probe (S3) presupposes the demo
  HONORS reduced-motion, which it does not yet (C.W3 lands it). W1 AUTHORS the
  probe + asserts it against the W3-honored build; the gate flips true once W3
  lands. This dependency is named in the spec — not silently coupled — so the
  close cannot read π as met before its precondition ships (inv ε).

- **inv β: execute disposition (a) — the no-legacy artefact.** RESOLVED: prefer
  regenerating the lockfile genuinely glass-ui-absent (a structural absence) over
  keeping the tolerated-dangling-link (b) with corrected prose. (a) is the
  no-legacy choice the mandate prefers; (b) is acceptable ONLY if (a) breaks the
  `file:` demo dev install, in which case the prose states the contingency
  exactly. Either way W6.md and FINAL.md are reconciled to the ONE shipped
  artefact — no contradictory branch survives.

- **The A11y=100 gate is honest-by-construction (tracked allowance, not silent
  exemption).** RESOLVED: the gate asserts the demo-owned closes at the
  open-panel state immediately; the glass-ui-blocked audits
  (`button-name`/`label`/`aria-input-field-name`, all from the `LabeledField`
  root cause, ASK-3) are a NAMED tracked allowance with an explicit removal
  trigger (glass-ui adoption), not a silent pass. The gate measures the product
  as USED (open panel), not the splash — fixing the scope error, not just the
  missing fixes. The glass-ui `LabeledField` defect routes OUTWARD (inv-16, ASK-3),
  never patched in the demo.
