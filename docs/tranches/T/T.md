   `SceneFacility`: every channel paints, the triad + elision DERIVE.

   `--spin-energy` filter flattens 3D; amiga's ValueUnit-boxed rotation NaNs the ball
   invisible; square's `"0pxpx"` CSSOM discard; compose's `:has()` height-0 collapse;
