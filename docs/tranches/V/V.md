## Thesis

One rail, two settlements, one truth pass. The rail (V.A) is externally gated
and everything demo-structural sequences behind it, so the settlement waves
never race the uncommitted K6/Glass-7 transaction. The two settlements (V.B
library, V.C demo) execute adjudicated blueprints — not fresh surveys — so
their waves are move tables with fences, cheap to verify and safe to
parallelize across disjoint trees. The truth pass (V.D/V.E) is deliberately
small: exactly four gates become real, everything else vacuous is deleted,
and the doc canon is corrected surgically with do-not-edit guards where the
audit refuted the inherited correction list. Design (V.F) and coordination
(V.G) ride the rendered, real-Glass demo the rail produces.
