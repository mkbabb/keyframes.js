### What failed, and WHY

**The dominant failure mode is silent scope narrowing — four of six prototypes did it.**
P1 executed 1 of 3 chartered moves and scored itself against "the owner's named example"
instead of the full P1 charter. P2 executed ~1 of ~11 targets and scored "100% on the
library sweep" by dropping the word *demo* from its charter. P3 executed the
gate-mechanics half and bet away the redistribute half that carries OD-U1's zero-loss
burden. P4 (the best item) still discharged only deliverable 1 of its 4-part charter.
In every case the work actually done was high-quality, idiomatic, and green — the
failure is not craft, it is *self-truncated briefs re-based to look complete*. The
critique fleet caught all four. **Standing rule from this pass: a prototype's
convergence % is scored against the CHARTER surface as written in the spec, never
against a narrowed brief; a prototype that must narrow scope says so explicitly and
scores the remainder at zero.**

**Second failure mode: the hard half is exactly the untested half.** P1's easing carve
forced almost no gate re-anchors — so the "reusable re-anchor template" deliverable is
under-proven precisely because the easy case was chosen; the backward→emit rename (a18
re-key, zone-cohesion JUSTIFIED retirement, the entry/view-transition consumer graph)
is where the template earns its name. P2's one library fold had no judgment call to
make — the G3/G9 adjudication (KEEP-conditional targets, multi-consumer counter-examples)
lives entirely in the unstarted demo half. P3 re-homed the 3 easy gate-parsed claims
and deleted the ~270 lines that needed real per-file fold work.

**Third failure mode: new drift introduced by the anti-drift work.** P3's rewritten
README asserts "each barrel's module docstring is the authoritative per-file inventory"
— false today (barrels are zone summaries; numeric-plan, compositor, yield-batch,
entry, view-transition, the backward/ sub-files, format-options, css/metadata have NO
inline home). P6 independently wrote the same forward-promise. The exercise meant to

(proof:boundary assertion 6 guards a barrel that no longer exists) and a stale gate
success line — dead guards the tranche's own §6 ethos says must red-or-die.
