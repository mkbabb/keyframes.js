## The load-bearing conclusion (why this band exists)

The owner's U prompt is **not a new tranche of new asks — it is the same 7-clause mandate
re-issued for the ~10th time** (`J/J.md:111-119` → verbatim through K,L,M,O,P,Q,R,S,T →
`U/ORIGINAL-PROMPT.md:14-16`), plus two escalations (CI-trim, grand-restructure). **The
recurrence is the finding** (lane 01 §0). A precept spirit-met to the owner's bar would not
need restating nine times; each re-issue **auto-REDs** its prior "ADDRESSED (chartered)" claim.

Every recap A→S measured LETTER and self-certified its SPIRIT column **by citing a band**
(→ S.G / → S.D / → S.E — the three bands the owner rejected on sight): "the anti-leak column
certified the leak." T cured half of this — `proof:prompt-recap-t` forbids band-as-oracle and
green-design-gate-as-oracle. **But T then reproduced the exact pathology one level down:**
verdict #26 (the GRAND COLOCATION EDICT) is marked LANDED · `proof:colocation` KEYSTONE ⑩
(`T/FINAL.md:44`), while on the live tree (verified `tranche-u-dev`, 5.2.0):

- `demo/@` EXISTS; `demo/shared` is ABSENT (T.F1 never ran).
- `demo/@/components/custom` EXISTS and holds ALL the good `instrument/` work (T.F2 never ran).
- `proof-colocation.mjs:50` is `SHARED = ["demo/@","demo/shared"].find(exists)` — tolerant by
  construction — with a DEFERRED map (`:70-89`) holding 3 live uncured violations, and the gate
  exits 0. **A keystone that cannot fail on the rejected structure is the blindspot with a
  certificate** (lane 03 F-2).

So the recap mechanism T built worked exactly where it looked and failed exactly where it did
not: the ledger's SPIRIT column was honest, but the STRUCTURAL keystone the ledger deferred to
was itself a tolerant green-on-rejected-tree gate. **U's transposition:** the recap ledger must
clear each ask against a TREE WITNESS the owner can read at first `ls` (or an owner token), never
against a wave/band mapping and never against a green source-shape gate for a design ask — AND

motion. This band owns the ledger + the gate renewal; U.B owns the keystone re-forge; the two are
DAG-coupled (the ledger's row-1 clearance IS U.B's owner-reviewed restructure).

The mechanism is **standing, not one-shot**: born at entry (now), updated at the close of each U
