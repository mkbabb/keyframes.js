## §Goal

Replace the five-authority lattice + the three competing playback authorities + the `isStableFire` double-fire heuristic with ONE `useSceneMachine()` (`createGlobalState` + a pure `transition(state, event)` reducer; states `idle | loading | playing | paused | suspended`) that OWNS the active-scene fact and the per-scene playback snapshot — done in ONE motion, the replaced surfaces *replaced*, not aliased beside (the spine: NO legacy beside its replacement). The route, the dock Select model, the `?anim=` param, and localStorage all become **read-only one-way projections** of `machine.activeScene` — killing the feedback loop at the source (the param/route can never write back into scene selection). Home and cube become DISTINCT states (home = no group, cube = its group — killing the alias). Suspend/restore becomes a declarative IDENTITY: a leave SUSPENDs (stops the active rAF, snapshots from the ONE authority), a `SCENE_READY` event RESTOREs on exactly one explicit transition (no remount race). Deep-link wins over localStorage. The deprecated `next()` drops to a returned value. The engine `AnimationGroup.serialize()/hydrate()` seam (the value-side of the demo) is named as a born-RED HANDOFF: the store lands FIRST against the existing imperative restore, then swaps to the engine API when it ships. This is the architectural transposition the spine names NECESSARY AND DESIRABLE — fewer composables (six → two), one source of truth per axis, the route storm and the corrupt panel and the orphaned rAF all DELETED by the single move.

## §Scope

- **S1 — `useSceneMachine()`: `createGlobalState` + a pure reducer (the §2.1 adjudicated facility).** One global `useSceneMachine()` on `createGlobalState`+`@vueuse/core` (the demo's existing standard — `controlOptionsStore.ts:35`, `useKeyboardShortcuts`), hosting a ~40-line typed pure `transition(state, event) → state` reducer. States `idle | loading | playing | paused | suspended`; context `{ activeScene: SceneId, perScene: Record<SceneId, PlaybackSnapshot> }`; events `NAVIGATE(to) · SCENE_READY · PLAY · PAUSE · SCRUB(t) · SUSPEND · RESUME · RESET` (+ the folded `TAB_HIDDEN/TAB_SHOWN` from `useSceneVisibilityPause`). The per-scene playback snapshot moves INTO this reactive store, collapsing the bare `Map` of `scenePlayback.ts` and merging the playback concern with the scene concern in ONE store keyed by scene (`a-scene-state-machine.md:184-216`; `a-demo-architecture.md:171-191`; `_SYNTHESIS-gap-scorecard.md:101-105`). **WHY:** the binding mandate forbids a parallel system; the demo already standardizes on `createGlobalState` — Pinia would be a second reactive-store paradigm (no-legacy violation) whose unique wins (devtools, persist plugin) are achievable here (`@vueuse/useStorage` gives persistence). - **S2 — collapse the THREE playback authorities to ONE (ST-1).** `useEasingDemo`'s private `isPlaying` ref AND its dummy `AnimationGroup` are DELETED — easing becomes a driver whose `RAFPlayback` loop gates on `machine.status === 'playing'` instead of a local ref; `usePlaybackToggle`'s SOLO/GROUPED fork COLLAPSES to one path (the machine is always the owner — "SOLO" only ever existed because there was no shared authority); `useAnimationGroupPlayback`'s `syncPlayState` routes through the machine action (`a-store-architecture.md:106,120-121`). - **`createGlobalState` + reducer, NOT Pinia — RESOLVED (the §2.1 adjudication, BINDING).** 2-of-3 D12 lanes favor `createGlobalState`+a hand-rolled reducer; the dissent's strongest point (devtools time-travel, persist plugin) is real but does not outweigh the spine's no-parallel-system rule.
