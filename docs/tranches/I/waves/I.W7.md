## §Scope

- **S1 — bind the GATE PRECEPT into the I charter (the precept, I-born).**
  > **A gate's ORACLE must be the PRODUCT PROPERTY a human would check, exercised through the
  > SAME surface the human uses, with an ERROR BUDGET OF ZERO across the human's interaction
  > battery (PLAY + SWITCH + DRAG). A gate whose oracle is source text, a jsdom unit, a
  > serialized snapshot, a self-captured baseline, a design-token number, or a paperwork ledger
  > is a HYGIENE gate, not a CORRECTNESS gate, and MUST be LABELED as such — it may never count
  > toward a correctness or chronic-closure tally.**

  The two-tier taxonomy IS the whole move: KEEP the 54 source-shape/jsdom gates — they are CHEAP
  and correctly police what their oracle CAN see (no-legacy held precisely because source text IS
  the right oracle for it). But STRIP them of correctness authority. Correctness and
  chronic-closure tallies may ONLY be satisfied by a gate whose oracle is the running product.
  **WHY:** a born-RED gate is only as honest as its oracle; the MORE rigorous the born-RED
  ceremony around a proxy oracle, the MORE false confidence it manufactures. The precept GOVERNS
  every prior wave's §Hard gate (each already obeys it — they CLICK, SWITCH, DRAG).

  **THE TAXONOMY APPLIES TO THE NEW GATES, NOT JUST THE RETIRED ONES — the overhaul holds itself
  to its own taxonomy (H-4 detail).** The two-tier rule is not only a re-labeling of H's 54

  meta-gates). For each new wave:

  - **Every config/lint/source-shape clause a new wave carries is strictly CORROBORATING and MUST

  The budget is NOT a bare "zero `console.error`"; that under-specifies what the battery accumulates
  and lets each wave drift its own console clause. Define it HERE, once, as a STRUCTURED ALLOWLIST,
  and have EVERY wave's console clause (I.W0–I.W6) inherit THIS definition by reference — never
  re-state a per-wave budget:

  | Tier | Signal | Threshold | Why |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **HARD** | `pageerror` (uncaught throw) | **= 0** | B1/B2's crashes ARE page errors; any is a correctness failure. |
  | **HARD** | `unhandledrejection` | **= 0** | an async throw that escapes is the same defect class. |
  | **HARD** | `console.error` | **= 0** | the Vue flush-abort warn + any logged error. |
  | **HARD** | value.js parse line `/Parse error at offset 0: "\.+"/` (the bare-`"......"` fingerprint) | **= 0** | the B1/B5 empty-input throw; matched explicitly so a narrowed regex can never hide it (`rc-parse-crash`). |
  | **PROMOTED** | `warning`/`verbose` matching `/ReadPixels\|GPU stall\|content-visibility\|hidden by content-visibility/` | **= 0** | NOT benign — these index a REAL GPU stall (RC-2); I.W3 clause (c) already promotes them past `error`-only because they measure the amiga per-frame ReadPixels stall (`rc-amiga §RC-2`, `I.W3.md` clause c). The session inherits the promotion: while the battery runs the amiga centre-drag leg, these `warning`/`verbose` lines RED the budget. |
  | **EXCLUDED (named-benign)** | the dev DevTools dep-optimizer source-map noise (B9-c, the ×47 `:5174`-only lines) | not counted | a dev-environment integrity artifact, explicitly accepted + documented at I.W5 (`rc-icons-build §1` — the built `dist/` is clean and never emits it). The dist legs never emit it, so the exclusion is inert there. |

  **The mechanism (no regex-narrowing escape hatch):** the budget is the COMPLEMENT of the named
  EXCLUDED set, not a positive match list — every captured `pageerror`/`unhandledrejection`/
  `console.error`/`warning`/`verbose` line that is NOT in the named-benign EXCLUDED set REDS the
  budget. This is the inversion of `proof:demo-console-clean`'s narrowed-regex sin (it asserted ONLY
  one `HA1_SIGNATURE` regex and filtered everything else OUT — so B1's flood passed, §The state).

## §Design decisions (trade-offs RESOLVED)

- **One driven session, not N rest-probes — RESOLVED.** The lattice of ~34 load-rest / wrong-
  projection browser gates each tested a fragment passively; ONE re-runnable interaction-driven
  battery (the investigation harness extended to actuate) is the KISS inversion. It bites every
  breakage at once with a single accumulated zero-error budget + product-facing DOM assertions. The
  per-wave §Hard gates are its clauses — authored per wave, assembled here.
- **Keep the source-shape gates, strip their correctness authority — RESOLVED.** The 54 source-
  shape/jsdom gates are NOT deleted — they are cheap and they correctly police what their oracle CAN
  see (no-legacy, zero-alloc). The move is the TWO-TIER taxonomy: they are labeled HYGIENE and may
  never count toward a correctness or chronic tally. Deleting them would lose real value; keeping
  them with false authority is the original sin. Re-tier, don't remove.
- **The visual baseline is re-grounded on known-good, not self — RESOLVED.** A self-captured,
  subject-masked baseline locks defects as golden (it locked the J1-J6 over-removal). The baseline
  must be a human-approved reference (or a prior-good tranche's render) and the live subject asserted
  ALIVE (a frame-delta proving motion), not painted pink. `proof:visual-lock` is downgraded to
  hygiene until re-grounded.
- **The chronic-closure meta-gate must police the PRODUCT — RESOLVED.** H made it a source-shape
  gate auditing other gates' paperwork (the deepest expression of the blindspot). The rewire
  requires each cited gate to be a runtime gate that BIT, forbids HANDOFF gates against unpublished
  targets, and forbids SYSTEM gates that measure the wrong axis. This is the durability keystone —
  it makes a chronic exit only via a gate that drove the live interaction.
- **ASSEMBLED LAST, but the PRECEPT is MECHANICALLY PRIOR — RESOLVED (RED-1).** The harden review's
  RED-1 is decisive: it is NOT enough for the overhaul to ASSERT "each prior wave already obeys the
  precept" and have it inherited backward by authorial fiat — that is the same "read the warning,
  re-commit the substance" failure H made. The lead reconciliation keeps the overhaul as the CLOSING
  wave (I.W7) because `proof:live-session`'s BATTERY is the union of W0-W6's runtime legs and is
  fully green only once they land — the ASSEMBLY genuinely happens last. BUT the gate-ORACLE PRECEPT
  is bound as a CHARTER INVARIANT from t=0 (in `I.md`'s invariant set, NON-wave) AND machine-enforced
  by `proof:gate-is-runtime` (S6) from t=0 — so a wave authored or run BEFORE the overhaul cannot
  ship a source-shape §Hard gate without the meta-gate reding. The precept is therefore MECHANICALLY
  prior (a charter invariant + a gate enforce it from the start), not merely stated-last-and-
  inherited-backward. What assembles last is the BATTERY; what governs from t=0 is the PRECEPT + its
  enforcer. (This is the structural answer the harden review demands: "the precept must be
  mechanically prior, not asserted-backward by the last wave" — `harden-review §RED-1`.)
- **CRITICAL, the headline — RESOLVED.** The catastrophe is not the nine breakages; it is that 97
  green gates certified them. Fixing the breakages without fixing the gate regime would let the next
  tranche re-paper them. The gate-regime overhaul is the ONLY deliverable that makes this the LAST
  re-paper — it is the headline the user mandated.
